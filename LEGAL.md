# Legal / Acceptable Use

**Not legal advice.** This document describes the project's design intent and
engineering hygiene.

## What this project is

`psarc2feedpak` is a **format converter**. It reads an already-unpacked
arrangement and writes the open `.feedpak` format for interoperability. It is
built to work on content **you lawfully own**, for **personal** format-shifting.

## What this project deliberately is not

- It does **not** decrypt files or circumvent any technological protection
  measure. Unpacking/decrypting a protected archive is a separate step you
  perform yourself, with separate general-purpose tooling, on content you own.
- It does **not** bundle, host, fetch, or run a decryptor, and it does not
  bundle, host, or link to any copyrighted content.

This separation is intentional: the converter is standalone and useful on any
unpacked arrangement, and it is not designed as part of a circumvention system.

## Your responsibility

You are responsible for the legality of your inputs and outputs in your
jurisdiction, including whether you may format-shift content you own and what
you do with the result. Do not use this tool to distribute copyrighted content.
